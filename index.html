How we work

From conversation to capital in four steps.

No 60-page applications. No black-box scoring. You'll talk to an actual underwriter who understands your industry and gives you a straight answer.

— 01

Apply

A short application, your A/R aging or purchase order, and basic financials. Most clients submit everything in under 30 minutes.

— 02

Review

We assess the strength of your customers and the structure of your deal — not just your credit score. Term sheet typically issued within 48 hours.

— 03

Onboard

Quick due diligence, lien filings, and account setup. Most facilities go from term sheet to first funding in 3–7 business days.

— 04

Fund

Submit invoices or POs as you generate them and receive funds the same day. Draw what you need, when you need it.
